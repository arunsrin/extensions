# Privacy Policy — TimeLedger

**Last updated:** 2026-11-27

TimeLedger is designed with strict local-only privacy:

1. **No Data Collection or Transmission:** TimeLedger does **not** transmit, upload, sell, or share any browsing history, URLs, page titles, timestamps, or usage statistics to any external server or third party.
2. **100% Local Storage:** All aggregated website/page durations and user preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API.
3. **Zero Network Requests:** TimeLedger makes zero background network requests, contains zero analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not query external favicon or font servers.
4. **User Control:** You can pause tracking at any time, exclude specific domains from tracking, export your data locally as JSON or CSV, or permanently delete all recorded data from the **Settings & Data** tab. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored extension data from your browser.
